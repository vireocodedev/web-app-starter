Adaptive client discovery

Procedure

Adaptive client discovery

Rounds and decisions

Model the interview as a decision tree with stable question IDs and explicit prerequisites. The frontier contains all unresolved questions whose prerequisites are settled. Ask that whole frontier in one round; a question dependent on another question in the same round belongs to a later round. Save partial replies and recompute the frontier. The tree can grow when answers reveal new branches.

Use this format and number continuously across the conversation:

❓ Q1 — Problem: Which recurring problem should this app solve, for whom,
and what happens today? Is this proposed description accurate: ...?
➡️ Recommended: ... because ...

❓ Q2 — Decision owners: Are you the person who can approve product scope,
and who will accept the completed app?
➡️ Recommended: record named responsibilities before accepting requirements.

Word a question so “yes” accepts the recommendation when a defensible recommendation exists. For unknown client facts, ask the open question, explain why it matters and say there is not enough evidence for a recommendation. Avoid invented client facts, forced defaults, fixed questionnaire length and repeat questions answered by sources. Ask applicable branches together without inventing answers to unblock others.

Inspect repository/source facts yourself after the initialization gate. Prefer available read-only connected sources for user-supplied references when authorized. Never ask the user to discover a fact the agent can inspect. Product intent, priorities, tradeoffs, business policy and approval belong to the user. A provided specification is source evidence; agent interpretations remain proposals until confirmed. Track conflicting statements by source ID and targeted question.

Coverage

The helper's fixed topic inventory is a completeness floor, not a questionnaire or proof of good analysis. Expand each applicable topic into real product-specific questions; record a rationale for every addressed or inapplicable topic. The core problem, users, outcomes, scope and acceptance always need substantive answers.

  • Problem, users, outcomes: current workarounds, pain and causes, beneficiaries, user groups, motivations, frequency and measurable success/failure indicators.
  • Scope: first release, future possibilities and explicitly excluded behavior; dependencies, essential versus optional capability and simpler alternatives.
  • Workflows and features: actors, triggers, preconditions, main path, alternate paths, permission failures, business rules, validation and observable outcomes.
  • Pages: purpose, entry points, navigation, displayed information, available actions and relevant loading/empty/error/success states; keyboard/focus behavior where meaningful. A page list alone is not a behavior specification.
  • Data: business entities, field meaning/type/requiredness, uniqueness, defaults, relationships and cardinality, ownership, lifecycle transitions, history, consistency rules, deletion and retention. Describe business structure; schema migrations, ORM models and API payload design belong to architecture planning.
  • Permissions: actors/roles, action and record scope, ownership changes, administrator behavior and access revocation. Check each included feature.
  • Business rules: calculations, limits, timing, approvals, cancellations, concurrent user expectations and dispute/error recovery.
  • Integrations and notifications: purpose, external owner, source of truth, consent, triggers, audiences/channels, retries, duplicates and failure behavior.
  • Search/reporting and import/export: visibility, filters/sorts, data volumes, formats, reporting definitions, partial errors and permission boundaries.
  • Localization/accessibility: languages, locale-specific formats, translated content ownership, user needs, keyboard/screen reader and accessibility targets.
  • Usage/performance: expected users, concurrent activity and data growth, important response times, availability and offline requirements where relevant.
  • Privacy/retention and migration: personal/sensitive data, permitted purposes, user-visible controls, retention/deletion obligations, existing data quality, mapping, ownership and acceptance. Record unresolved specialist/legal questions with owners; do not invent legal conclusions.
  • Visual direction/responsive behavior: references, branding/assets, navigation expectations, page layouts, devices, narrow-screen priorities and interaction preferences. Mockups and coded prototypes are separate tasks.
  • Constraints/responsibilities: deadline/budget constraints without fabricated estimates, external dependencies, who supplies content/data, product decision authority and final acceptance responsibility.
  • Acceptance: measurable positive, negative and recovery scenarios for the included workflows, roles and rules, plus who accepts them and on what evidence.

Ask why a requested feature helps the agreed outcome, who needs it and whether a simpler option would meet the need. Challenge contradictions respectfully; retain the user's resulting decision even when it differs from your recommendation.

Completion and change

A topic label marked addressed is not sufficient evidence of depth. Review the actual answers and documents together. Every included feature must have actor, trigger, normal path, rules/validation, failure behavior and acceptance; every page/data relation/permission must agree across documents. Track stable IDs and source/question references. Missing included behavior remains blocking.

“Decide later” is unresolved when included functionality depends on the answer. Future/excluded questions may remain deferred with rationale and no first-release dependency. Revisit scope when a blocker is explicitly moved outside the release; never silently relabel it nonblocking to pass validation.

Save after each answer batch. On resume show a short recap and ask only unresolved frontier questions. New source material, conflicts and changed answers reopen relevant branches. For approved work, first create a revision with the helper; present its impact on features, pages, data and acceptance before reapproval.

Procedure

Consumer initialization contract

The execution skill owns running setup. The sibling intake skill owns the complete upfront questionnaire, saved answers and completed handoff. The runbook owns exact application edits and links to operator procedures.

Supported scope and target

Initialize one existing private GitHub consumer with one Git worktree containing frontend/ and backend/. Preserve the template stack, session authentication, examples, shared Hetzner CI and paired staging/production release workflows. Reuse the approved operator, shared App/pool, deployment host and supported backup destination. Creating a new foundation, changing privileged shared code, rebuilding a host, replacing data and implementing business features are separate tasks. Canonical vireocodedev/web-app-starter cannot initialize itself.

Intake handoff and authority

Execution's first operation is the intake require-complete gate. Missing, partial, invalid, changed or repository-mismatched intake stops execution with an explicit instruction to finish $initialization-intake first. A legacy workbook or old execution state is not a completed new intake. Local dry-run intake is practice only: its separate state and rehearsalComplete cannot unlock execution.

Read the state contract. The helper derives readiness from valid applicable answers and prerequisites, and binds confirmation to their exact content and repository. A mutable complete: true flag alone is insufficient. Record the returned intake revision and digest in execution state; recheck them on resume and before phase writes. Answer changes require refreshed intake confirmation, while preserving already verified execution evidence and existing resources.

All setup choices, scope authorization and foreseeable human prerequisites are collected during intake. Use only actual user answers/consent; the agent can record their words but cannot approve for them. The current execution request and the completed confirmed scope must agree. A saved file grants no standing authority for unrelated resources or new actions. Credentials stay in protected stores; intake/state contain references only. No Client Discovery service, project UUID, export, app review or external workbook is required.

The intake's protected external operator reference identifies the existing operator. Resolve it as data, not commands. Keep the JSON file outside Git, owner-only (0600 in private directories). It contains references, never token, password or private-key values:

From approved scope to bounded issues

Procedure

From approved scope to bounded issues

Read the copied public specification, relevant repository guides and current source examples. Reuse actual capability owners; filenames or existing screen names alone do not establish the right boundary. Source approval settles functional requirements, not new architecture choices or permission to implement them.

Cover each included feature, page/workflow, data rule and acceptance scenario. Use a compact coverage table in the package README when it helps expose omissions. Future/excluded requirements remain outside the implementation backlog. An issue may reference them to explain a boundary, never to silently include them.

Choose tasks by a coherent observable outcome that a reviewer can assess. Include the supporting UI/API/data/locale/docs work needed for that outcome where applicable; avoid automatically splitting every feature into one issue per technical layer. Use epic grouping when multiple independently reviewable outcomes share a goal. An epic has aggregate acceptance and explicit children, not a second implementation of the same work. Parent grouping does not imply blocking. dependsOn records only genuine prerequisites. Define owners as capability/responsibility boundaries, not guessed GitHub assignees.

Every body contains nonempty sections:

## Outcome

The observable user or developer result.

## Scope

Included behavior and explicit boundaries, including applicable failure paths.

## Acceptance criteria

- Observable success, validation, permissions and failure scenarios from the approved source.

## Verification

Relevant existing checks and manual acceptance, with any later planning needed.

## Decisions

Source-backed settled choices and any separately owned technical planning needs.

## Source

Approved document names and stable requirement/acceptance IDs.

## Dependencies

Local issue IDs for actual blockers; state None when there are none.

Make these sections specific to the issue. The helper checks structure and exact source references, while the agent reviews semantic coverage and meaningful acceptance. Missing requirement IDs must be added through the specification owner and approved; don't edit the retained snapshot. A missing product rule returns the affected source branch to specification revision instead of inventing policy.

Resolve routine technical detail only within documented patterns and delegated authority. Where architecture needs a consequential decision, write a bounded planning issue with its questions, inputs, decision owner and expected reviewable result; make dependent implementation issues explicit. Default issues to needs-triage. ready-for-agent requires actual implementation readiness and the repository's decision process, not functional approval alone.

Use canonical triage labels and meanings from docs/agents/triage-labels.md. Label definitions allow the importer to create exact missing labels if authorized; they do not authorize changing existing labels. Keep secrets, raw interview answers, source originals and actual approval words outside the package. Review sanitization even when the heuristic credential check passes.

Procedure

Full initialization completion receipt

Use this contract after executing and verifying the full initialization phases, or when reconciling historical consumer setup before specification creation. The helper records observed evidence; it performs no provider operations and does not establish that an operator's observations are truthful or still live.

Record observed full execution

Prepare a private JSON input (0600, outside Git) with these exact top-level fields: sourceCommit, phases, deployments, and recovery. Use actual consumer observations. Run from the selected consumer:

node .agents/skills/initialization-execution/scripts/completion.mjs complete --root /absolute/consumer --input /private/completion-evidence.json

--input - accepts JSON through stdin. The producer first requires completed full intake for the actual GitHub consumer. It rejects local-only scope and the canonical template. sourceCommit is the exact 40-character initialized, tested and deployed consumer commit; it must be available in local Git history and an ancestor of the selected checkout. Later specification commits may follow.

For each of the nine phases, supply {"status":"passed","evidence":{"checkId":"private:opaque-reference"}}. The authoritative required check IDs are exported as completionPhases in completion.mjs. Each required observation needs its own meaningful reference to the actual check, run or protected operator record. Supported references are this consumer's GitHub Actions run URLs, repository-relative docs/ or infrastructure/ evidence paths, and opaque private: identifiers resolved through protected operating guidance. References are not secrets, log excerpts, shell commands or approvals. The schema checks their shape; the executing agent must inspect the actual evidence privately.

deployments contains staging and production, each with exactly:

FieldValue
urlCredential-free HTTPS origin; staging and production use distinct hostnames.
runUrlActual consumer GitHub Actions run URL, optionally with /attempts/N.
sourceCommitThe same initialized and tested commit as the receipt.
frontendghcr.io/<owner>/<repo>-frontend@sha256:<64 lowercase hex characters>.
backendghcr.io/<owner>/<repo>-backend@sha256:<64 lowercase hex characters>.

The production image references must equal the accepted staging pair exactly. recovery contains exactly initialized: true, isolated: true, backupSha256, retrievedSha256, and restoredSha256. All three checksums must be the same 64-character SHA-256 of the initialized production archive. Phase evidence additionally identifies provisioning, restored schema/data checks, scratch cleanup, and enabling the production timer after the successful restore. The release operator remains the owner of the actual recovery procedure.

Files and integrity

Successful recording writes sanitized docs/acceptance/initialization-acceptance.json with schema version 1, actual repository, full scope, source commit, completed intake revision/digest, completion timestamp, structured evidence and canonical content digest. It also writes an owner-only binding at .template-initialization/completion.json containing repository, canonical worktree root, source commit and matching digest. The private directory is 0700, its marker 0600, and it remains Git-ignored.

The producer uses an exclusive lock, no-follow regular-file checks and individual atomic, flushed file publications. The private binding is published last: an interrupted pair is incomplete and cannot unlock specification. Inspect surviving evidence and any stale lock before an explicit retry. Existing completion markers are preserved and require deliberate reconciliation before replacement. Older unstructured acceptance documents also need actual evidence reconciliation; they are not automatically converted or treated as a new completed receipt.

The digest detects accidental changes and mismatched files. It is not a signature, provider attestation or authorization to mutate infrastructure. Keep private answers, credentials, customer rows and credential-bearing logs out of receipts. Secret-like strings are rejected heuristically; inspect every public reference.

Gate specification creation

node .agents/skills/initialization-execution/scripts/completion.mjs require-complete --root /absolute/consumer

This offline, read-only gate checks the receipt schema/integrity, all phase evidence, image/recovery consistency, the actual Git origin, private binding, safe owned paths and historical source ancestry. It returns only repository, scope, source commit, acceptance path and digest. Its module interface is requireFullInitialization(root).

Missing, partial, local-only, malformed, edited, foreign or inconsistent evidence fails nonzero and explicitly directs the user to finish $initialization-execution before $specification-creator. New specification work may not repair or fabricate receipts to bypass this gate.

The gate validates historical initialization, not current HEAD equality or current intake completion. Later application/specification commits and intake changes do not erase valid past acceptance. A moved checkout, missing private binding, shallow/rewritten history or a renamed origin requires explicit reconciliation of actual evidence; it does not authorize reinitializing or replacing resources. assessFullInitializationReceipt(receipt) exposes pure schema/integrity checks for public documentation rendering; it is not the complete worktree gate.

Functional specification output

Write reviewable, sanitized Markdown under docs/specification/. Start with SPECIFICATION.md; add supporting files only when they improve review, such as FEATURES.md, PAGES.md, DATA.md, DECISIONS.md and ACCEPTANCE.md. The helper accepts simple uppercase Markdown filenames, not arbitrary output paths. Keep the entry point concise with working relative links to the supporting detail.

The specification describes the problem, users, outcomes, first release, future possibilities, exclusions, functional behavior, constraints and responsibilities. Use stable feature/page/entity/decision/acceptance IDs and source/question references so changes and contradictions can be followed across documents. Identify actual source statements, confirmed user decisions, proposals and unresolved questions. A requirement table or matrix can expose missing acceptance, data or permission coverage; it cannot replace the behavior descriptions.

Describe data at the business level: meaning, fields, cardinality, ownership, lifecycle, invariants and deletion. Reuse and link root CONTEXT.md as the glossary owner; do not create a competing glossary or nested CONTEXT.md. Follow the consumer's domain-modeling instructions when a real glossary update is needed. Record conflicts with existing ADRs for a separately authorized architecture decision. Keep technical architecture, endpoint design, SQL migrations and implementation backlog outside this skill's outputs.

Drafts clearly identify incompleteness. Before approval, review cross-document consistency and verify links, source/decision traceability and the completeness of included workflows. The helper checks state structure, prerequisites, topic coverage and hashes. The agent and client retain responsibility for semantic accuracy, useful acceptance and product fit. Never treat a machine check as approval.

Record the actual human approval words only in private state. Public version receipts contain repository, revision, digest, timestamp and document hashes. versions/approved-NNN/ preserves the exact approved Markdown before later edits; those snapshots remain reviewable and can be version controlled. Private approved interview snapshots remain in the ignored state directory.

Keep source originals in their existing protected location and retain their content identity in private state. Never copy confidential notes, interview state, credentials or private source paths into public dashboards. The skill does not stage, commit, publish issues or modify remote systems. Initialization acceptance, requirements approval and acceptance of implemented software remain distinct.

Downstream issue handoff

It must use require-approved and copy only the exact public approved snapshot, not private interview state. A missing business rule returns to this specification's revision workflow. Neither step is implicit in specification approval, and imported tickets do not report implemented behavior.

Procedure

Import contract

Commands and authority

From the consumer checkout with Node 24/npm 12:

node .agents/skills/github-issues-import/scripts/import.mjs plan --package docs/issue-packages/v1
node .agents/skills/github-issues-import/scripts/import.mjs apply --package docs/issue-packages/v1 --repo OWNER/REPO

plan is offline: it validates the sibling specification-to-issues package, approved copied source and current source when a consumer root exists, resolves the target against Git origin, and prints labels/order/relationships. It writes neither checkpoints nor remote resources. apply requires the user's import instruction naming that package and repository. The CLI flag identifies the target; it does not prove conversational authorization. A bare skill invocation waits for a selected task.

--root /absolute/consumer selects another checkout. The CLI otherwise detects the current Git root. Outside a checkout, a portable copied package requires --repo OWNER/REPO and verifies its copied approval/source evidence. It cannot establish whether that source has subsequently changed in its original checkout. In a checkout, a differing origin or no current approval stops the import.

Published content and labels

One Markdown body becomes one issue, preserving its bytes and adding a deterministic package/item marker and readable approved-source iteration, revision, digest and references. The reserved <!-- issue-package namespace belongs to this importer. Duplicate/spoofed markers or title/body drift stop publication. GitHub issue database IDs, numbers and exact target URLs are distinct values; relationships use database IDs.

Existing labels retain their color/description. Only missing labels declared by the validated package are created. Existing issue labels/lifecycle are preserved. The packaging owner defaults new issues to needs-triage; functional approval is not coding authorization. This importer does not publish comments, grant implementation readiness, assign users, close/reopen/delete issues or replace manual edits.

Blockers are created before their dependents. Parent grouping has its own graph and is attached only after all issue numbers exist; parent grouping does not imply blocking. Native sub-issue and blocked-by endpoints are the default. If these capabilities are unavailable, explicitly select --relationship-mode body-links to publish an importer-owned relationship section containing parent, child and blocker URLs. The selected mode is pinned in the checkpoint. Authentication, validation and rate-limit failures stop; they never silently switch modes. Closed issues are reused and preserved, including their existing bodies and relationships; report any relationships intentionally skipped for those records.

Resume and conflicts

Private checkpoints live under this skill's ignored state/<repository-hash>/<package-id>.json. Atomic writes, fsync, private modes and an exclusive local lock protect the original repository, package digest, relationship mode, mappings and per-action intent/outcome. Pending intent is durable before every remote mutation. Packages and consumer origin are checked again before each mutation.

Resume enumerates all open/closed issues, excludes pull requests and reconciles exact stable markers. A single marker with the expected title/body recovers a successful create whose response was lost. Multiple matches, missing previously mapped issues and manual title/body edits stop. Importer-owned body changes accept only the recorded before/after variants while pending. Successful imports can be rerun without remote writes.

A pending mutation whose result cannot be observed remains uncertain. Preserve it and inspect/reconcile GitHub rather than automatically retrying a create. The same conservative rule applies to ambiguous label or relationship requests. A definitive rejection can be diagnosed from the bounded HTTP status, but this helper intentionally keeps its original intent until an operator resolves it; deleting checkpoints is not a retry procedure. Stale locks require checking active writers first.

A local lock does not coordinate other machines. Concurrent imports from another machine or manual changes between a final read and write remain possible. Use one publisher for a package/target. The GitHub API has no transaction spanning all tickets; successful earlier actions remain when later steps stop. No live GitHub import is required when developing this skill: tests inject a fake transport.

Maintainer API references

The transport uses bounded gh api argument lists and JSON stdin, configured gh authentication and GitHub API version 2026-03-10; it never places credentials in arguments or reports raw stderr. Providers must return the same repository identity, exact issue URLs, valid database IDs and bounded paginated lists. Network failures and malformed responses after writes retain uncertainty.

  • GitHub CLI API
  • Issue creation and retrieval
  • Repository labels
  • Sub-issues
  • Issue dependencies

These helpers implement the repository's tracker conventions and triage roles. Validate an import using its exact package, source approval, target, real mappings and observed relationship mode. Passing mock tests establishes local behavior only.

Select body-links before the initial apply when native capabilities are unavailable. An existing native checkpoint cannot simply switch modes: preserve its mappings and pending intents and resolve the capability failure with a bounded, explicitly reviewed reconciliation. Deleting the checkpoint or silently changing modes can lose evidence of earlier successful writes.

Save answers and documents

init preserves an existing interview. New state has revision 0, iteration 1 and an unresolved inventory of 24 topic families (the topicIds export). A save merges named entries, increments revision and writes a draft. Entries are whole records, not deep patches. Changing a previously settled question reopens its topic and all transitive dependent questions/topics. Even dependent answers included in the same batch are reopened: reconfirm them in a later round after the prerequisite settles. Unrelated decisions remain intact. Reassess affected topic coverage before validation. Example:

{
  "expectedRevision": 0,
  "questions": {
    "problem_statement": {
      "topic": "problem",
      "prompt": "What recurring problem does the app solve?",
      "recommendation": "Keep the first release focused on the agreed recurring problem.",
      "dependsOn": [],
      "status": "answered",
      "answer": "Coordinators need one shared view of appointment availability.",
      "provenance": "user",
      "sourceIds": [],
      "blocking": true,
      "decisionEvidence": "The user confirmed that coordinators and availability are the initial focus."
    }
  },
  "topics": {
    "problem": {
      "status": "addressed",
      "rationale": "Problem, current workaround and affected users were confirmed.",
      "questionIds": ["problem_statement"]
    }
  }
}

Question IDs are stable lowercase identifiers. Dependencies must exist and form an acyclic graph. status is unknown, proposed, answered, deferred or not_applicable; provenance is user, source or proposal. Non-unknown records need a nonempty answer/rationale. Answered analyst proposals are rejected. User answers and inapplicability decisions need actual decision evidence; source-derived answers need source IDs. Preserve unanswered questions as unknown, not guessed answers. Every conflict is a blocking question referencing conflicting source IDs.

Sources are a map such as:

{
  "client_notes": {
    "kind": "file",
    "reference": "/protected/location/client-notes.md",
    "sha256": "the-64-character-lowercase-SHA256-of-the-exact-source-bytes"
  }
}

Use kind: conversation for actual message evidence, with a stable reference and digest of the retained statement. Compute hashes from the actual contents, not a file name or mutable URL. The helper checks reference integrity and digest shape; the agent must check source bytes on adoption/resume, flag changes and preserve previously reviewed source identities. Source IDs are immutable: changed source contents require a new source ID. Source edits do not authorize silently updating requirements. Ask whether to revise and trace the affected branches.

Topic status is unresolved, addressed or not_applicable, with a rationale and the question IDs covering it. Addressed topics require settled questions; inapplicable topics require a rationale and settled referenced questions. Problem, users, outcomes, scope and acceptance cannot be inapplicable. Unknown/proposed/ deferred blocking questions and settled questions with unsettled prerequisites prevent readiness. Deferred future/excluded questions can be nonblocking with explicit rationale; semantic review must confirm included behavior does not depend on them. The machine cannot prove that a topic's answers are sufficiently detailed.

A documents patch maps uppercase Markdown filenames to their full UTF-8 content. It writes only docs/specification/ and records exact hashes. SPECIFICATION.md is required for readiness. Manual edits cause a manifest mismatch and are preserved; review and adopt them using a separate save with adoptDocuments: true before further output changes. An unexpected document inventory or an interrupted partial write requires explicit reconciliation. Recheck links and semantic coverage after adoption. The helper does not overwrite manual edits automatically.

Approval and revisions

validate succeeds only with complete structural coverage, no blocking questions and a matching document manifest. status/validate return the current revision, reviewDigest and documentHashes. Present the exact revision and documents for human review. Approval input must bind those exact reviewed values:

{
  "expectedRevision": 12,
  "reviewedDigest": "the-reviewDigest-returned-for-revision-12",
  "reviewedDocuments": { "SPECIFICATION.md": "its-exact-SHA256" },
  "confirmation": {
    "response": "The actual human response approving this reviewed specification."
  }
}

approve increments revision, records actual approval words in private state and binds the receipt to the state and exact Markdown hashes. It also preserves a private state/approved-NNN.json and public docs/specification/versions/approved-NNN/ Markdown snapshot with a sanitized receipt. Both snapshots are required later; they are historical evidence, not a signature or proof that the agent recorded the human faithfully. require-approved rejects changed state/documents/snapshots. Public receipts never include interview answers, private source paths or approval words. No commit or publication occurs.

An approved interview rejects save; begin a change with:

{
  "expectedRevision": 13,
  "affectedQuestionIds": ["problem_statement"],
  "reason": "The actual request changing the target users or their problem."
}

revise verifies preserved approval snapshots, increments iteration/revision, reopens the specified questions and all transitive dependents, marks their topics unresolved, and preserves unaffected answers. It captures current manual document edits without erasing the approved snapshot. The current draft loses approval; resolve affected branches, update the relevant requirements and obtain approval again. New branches can then be added through normal save. Add explicit dependency edges before approval so impact propagation reflects the real product relationships.

Credential scanning is heuristic, not proof that free text is safe. Keep credentials in protected storage, review output sanitization, and confirm ignored state is not staged. Never force-add private state or include it in dashboards.
